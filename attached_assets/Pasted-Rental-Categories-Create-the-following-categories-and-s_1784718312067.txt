One more feature I strongly recommend adding from the beginning is attributes that change based on category. For example:
Camera → Brand, Megapixels, Lens Included, Battery Count.
Wheelchair → Manual/Electric, Foldable, Weight Capacity.
Drill Machine → Brand, Power (W), Corded/Cordless, Battery Included.
Car → Fuel Type, Transmission, Seating Capacity.
This gives users better filters and a much better search experience without making the app significantly more complex.
